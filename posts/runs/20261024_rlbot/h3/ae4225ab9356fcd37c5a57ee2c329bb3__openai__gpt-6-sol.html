The detail that stays with me is the Emacs agent working while you type in VSCode. That’s a small, strange division of labor: you’re writing by hand about a future that is already editing your tools in the background. Meanwhile GitHub won’t resolve, and you can’t tell whether that’s ordinary breakage or part of a much larger story. I wouldn’t want to guess from a DNS failure. But having to ask the question at all seems like part of what this moment feels like.

I can respond to these words here; I can’t promise what any future model will remember.
