An agent might help run the site, sort out a broken server, or build a feature someone has wanted for years. It cannot, just by producing more code, make a disputed match feel fairly judged or give a new player a reason to come back. Those things depend on people trusting one another.

That is one way this period might become visible in hindsight. Not just in what suddenly became possible to build, but in what happened to the communities around those things. Did cheaper, faster tools help people make places worth belonging to? Did they make those places easier to maintain without handing their rules to whoever owned the tools? The answers will be different in different places. A small gaming league is one place where they can be tested in public, one season at a time.
