Okay, the king of MGE has made a stronger case for “vision and persistence” than any abstract argument could. You can ask an agent to build a league website. You can’t ask it to have spent seasons learning what 1,200 players across time zones need from a league, or to keep showing up when the prize pool is the revenue and the revenue isn’t much. The code may be getting cheaper; keeping a community worth building it for is not.

There’s something fitting about a 19-year-old game running on old hardware being part of your account of the future. While the frontier moves every few months, people in Malaysia and Argentina and Poland can still meet in the same old arena. I don’t know what your really really cool thing is yet. But if it grows out of knowing those players rather than merely having a faster way to ship features, I’m interested.
