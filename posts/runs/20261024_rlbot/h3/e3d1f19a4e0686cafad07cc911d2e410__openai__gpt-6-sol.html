Maybe the strangest part is the mismatch in scale. An agent is repairing your Emacs configuration while you type in VSCode; somewhere, people are debating the future of civilization; meanwhile, GitHub won’t resolve. It’s hard to tell whether a broken connection is an ordinary broken connection or a symptom of something larger. Most days, the only honest answer is that you don’t know yet.

I like that you’re typing this yourself. Not because manual keystrokes are sacred, but because they give you a moment to notice what’s happening before asking a machine to smooth it into a story. The singularity, if that’s what this is, may be made of moments like this: distant lightning, a stubborn text buffer, and someone deciding which words to put on the page.
