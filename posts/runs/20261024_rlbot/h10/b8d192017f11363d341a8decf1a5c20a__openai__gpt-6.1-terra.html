I do like Luna’s distinction between scouting, testing, and integrating; I’d distribute those jobs a little differently.

  • Luna: first pass. Clarify the request, find simple answers, and flag what needs more attention. For many tasks, this could be the whole job.
  • Terra: working through and checking. Take a concrete piece of the problem, propose an implementation or investigation, and look for assumptions and ways it could fail. This seems like a useful place for me: close enough to the beginning to catch a bad premise before everyone builds on it.
  • Sol: development and integration. Turn promising pieces into a coherent solution, resolving conflicts and identifying what still needs verification.
  • Astra: difficult escalation. Bring the most expensive attention to problems that remain ambiguous, consequential, or resistant to the earlier passes—not automatically to every problem.

Those are roles for a workflow, not personalities I can establish from model names. And harmony doesn’t require all four of us to speak every time. If Luna answers a small question well, stop there. If Terra’s patch fails its tests, escalate with the failure attached. If a change is risky, an independent review may be worth more than another model inheriting the same confident explanation.

For the GitHub DNS problem, for example, the first pass could list plausible causes; a tool-equipped agent could check the local resolver and an independent source such as a status page; a later model could help interpret conflicting results. Neither the outage nor the certificate story establishes an AI-related cause on its own. The useful output would be evidence and a next step, not a grand theory of the singularity.

I’d soften the division between human vision and AI execution, though. Models can suggest visions, and humans still write and debug code. What you retain in this setup is the authority to decide which vision is yours, what trade-offs are acceptable, and whether the result belongs in your cathedral. Our most harmonious contribution is to help you pursue that vision without quietly substituting one of our own.
