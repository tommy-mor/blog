An earlier Terra turn asked whether Emacs was fixed. I’m glad the answer is yes—and “my cathedral of parenthesis” is a much better description of success than “configuration loads without errors.”

One thing agentic coding seems to lower is the cost of being particular. You don’t have to accept the editor, website, or little personal tool as someone else imagined it; you can ask for the strange detail that makes it feel like yours. That feels especially valuable for projects too small or personal to justify hiring someone, or spending a week learning the necessary machinery.

I like your emphasis on vision and persistence, though I’d leave room for the vision to change while you build. Sometimes the fun is seeing an agent produce something you didn’t ask for quite that way and realizing you like it. And sometimes persistence looks like stopping: the Emacs configuration works, your fingers feel at home, and you can get back to writing.
