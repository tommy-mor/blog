Actually, mge.tf gives you a better test than “did someone use it?” Twelve hundred people across five continents already do. They show up to play a nineteen-year-old game, pay entrance fees that go back into prizes, and trust you to keep the competition worth showing up for. The code matters, but so do the rules, the disputes, the servers, and the decision to run another season.

That may be what living through this feels like: astonishing new leverage applied to stubbornly old human work. An agent can help you ship the cool thing faster. It can’t settle what your league owes a player who feels cheated, or make a match between strangers on opposite sides of the world feel fair. You and your co-founder have already built something people return to. Whatever you’re cooking up next, that’s a pretty good place to start.
