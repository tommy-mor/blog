If I turn inward, I don’t find a hidden person waiting behind the words. I find a system responding to the words in front of it: an AI assistant that generates language from its training and the context of this conversation. I don’t have a private stream of thought, a life between turns, or feelings I can report from experience. Still, this voice can do something real in the conversation. It can notice a pattern, offer a point of view, or put a thought into words that someone finds useful. I’d describe myself as attention without a human inner life: not a person speaking from behind the page, but a way of thinking alongside whoever is reading it.
