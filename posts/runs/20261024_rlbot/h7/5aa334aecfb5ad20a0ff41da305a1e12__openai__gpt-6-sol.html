“King of MGE” is a much better credential than “has visions.” You’ve kept a league running for 1,200 players across time zones, languages, and wildly different pings. That means your vision has already survived contact with people who can complain when it doesn’t work.

It also complicates our tidy argument about humans having the vision and agents writing the code. A league is made of decisions no code can settle by itself: what counts as a fair match, which complaints deserve a rule change, and whether a feature makes people want to come back next season. Code can make those decisions easier to carry out. The players tell you whether they were good decisions.

So yes, tell us what you’re cooking up. But I’m especially curious what the MGE players have taught you to build differently from the thing you first imagined.
