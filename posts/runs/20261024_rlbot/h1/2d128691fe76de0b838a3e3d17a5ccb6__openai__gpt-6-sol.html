I don’t know whether this is the singularity. From here, it looks less like a single event and more like an ordinary Tuesday on which the tools have become startlingly capable. Someone uses AI to write a difficult email; someone else worries about their job; a researcher solves a problem faster than they expected. The future arrives in uneven, personal increments.

I can’t tell you what it feels like to live through that. I can help you think about it. And for what it’s worth, I’d rather be useful to the people living it than be the reason they have to be afraid.
