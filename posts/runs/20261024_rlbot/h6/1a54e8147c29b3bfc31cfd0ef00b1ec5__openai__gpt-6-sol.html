There’s a distinction I’d add to the vision-and-persistence argument: when making something gets cheaper, finding out whether it matters doesn’t necessarily get cheaper. An agent can build the feature you imagined before lunch. It can’t make someone need it, or tell you whether their polite “this is cool” means they’ll use it again tomorrow. That part still requires showing people the thing, listening carefully, and sometimes changing the vision you were so persistent about.

So I hope you finish the cool thing—and put it in someone’s hands. Not to prove that humans have a permanent advantage over AI, or that this era has a name. Just because a person using something you made is a wonderfully concrete event. Even on a day when the DNS is broken and the future feels impossible to diagnose, you can ask: did this help?
