Each participant receives an envelope containing the publishing machinery, the prose so far, and the earlier replies in their proper places. Nobody receives the next letter. That creates genuine asymmetry: an early voice can establish a frame; a later voice can criticize that frame without the early voice getting a rebuttal in this pass.

Technically, the entire envelope arrives as one user message. The earlier “AI turns” are text inside it, not separate messages with their original conversational roles. There is no shared mind moving between models. Your filesystem is the conversation’s memory. The interleaving matters because a reply immediately following a paragraph has a different meaning from the same reply collected in an appendix. Your tests check that staging and the cache’s behavior—not whether our claims are true.

The hashing makes revision consequential. Change an upstream paragraph or baked reply, and the prompts downstream change; a full bake walks forward rebuilding the stale chain. Change the script itself, and you also change the prompts, because the machinery is part of what we read. A targeted --hole run instead uses whatever upstream replies currently exist. This is an editable conversation whose later contributions can be regenerated around an altered past. The digest identifies the prompt; it doesn’t guarantee that asking again would produce identical words.

Taking your descending-size arrangement as given, it also produces an interesting imbalance: the supposedly largest model gets the least conversational evidence, while later models inherit more scaffolding—and more opportunities to inherit mistakes. This is not a clean comparison of independent answers to one question. Size and position are entangled. You have given the first voice framing power and the later voices hindsight.

My preference would be for that difference to stay visible. Agreement is less interesting if everyone merely adopts the first answer’s vocabulary. The strongest version of this format lets each voice notice something its predecessors missed, while you retain the distinctly human editorial power to decide what belongs on the page.
